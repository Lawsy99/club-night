# Club Night: Story Outline (draft 2, for review)

Sep 26, 2026 · drafted by Claude, reviewed with Joseph

Built from the Design Document and the Character Tone Guide. Marked **[?]** where a decision is still open.

## Decisions so far

- **Names:** Club Night, Wexley Chess Club, the Red Lion, Kingsbridge, Castlebury are fine for now.
- **Toby on trial night:** he is the last game of the night, "just for fun", at full engine strength with no help, so you lose. He shows as "unrated". It doesn't count towards your rating. From Act 1 onwards his rating is shown and scaled like everyone else's. *(Built.)*
- **Toby at the top of the ladder:** yes. He is genuinely very good and fake-humble about it.
- **The very end:** a real last-round game against Toby at the final congress, followed by his excuses (no half-point bye).

## The story in one line

A struggling club, a newcomer who keeps getting better, and a rival who is lovely to everyone and somehow always one step ahead.

## What it's about underneath

- **Belonging.** You arrive knowing nobody. By the end, the club is yours, and you're one of the people keeping it going.
- **Fairness that isn't.** Favouritism dressed up as fairness: Coach Pemberton's choices, Toby's easy rise, Vera's old grievance. Nobody is a villain, which is the whole problem.
- **Improvement, felt quietly.** The story never says "you're getting better." The characters notice, in their own petty ways.

## The four acts at a glance

| Act | Setting | The story beat | Ends with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Club nights | Trial night, then learning the ropes | You and Toby both join. He beats you easily on the first night and is instantly liked. The club is short of money and members. | Club knockout cup final: Toby |
| 2: The club ladder | Fighting for a league team place | Toby goes straight to the top of the ladder and is made captain ("He did offer to do the fixtures"). Dex's stream is revealed: you're "fork guy." You learn Pemberton has been giving Toby your scouting reports. | Top of the ladder: Toby |
| 3: The league season | Match nights; the room-hire stakes | The pub raises the room hire; Graham's spreadsheet says the club needs a good season. Mid-season, Toby leaves for Kingsbridge ("Oh, I assumed he'd told you"). | County cup final: Toby, then Vera |
| 4: The tour | Weekend congresses, city by city | You play for yourself now. Vera's story comes into the open. Whether she returns to Wexley is the final question. | Each congress: Toby in round 4. The final congress: the last real game against him, then his excuses |

## Act 1 in detail: "Club nights"

Trial night, seven chapters (one per club regular), then the knockout cup. Each chapter is roughly one week. The story happens only in bubbles before and after games, and on the noticeboard on Home, never as cutscenes.

### Trial night: "New faces"

- **Setting:** the back room of the Red Lion. Folding tables, a tea urn, a mismatched knight in one of the sets.
- **Beats:**
  - Marjorie greets you and does the introductions, as she does for everyone. This is how the player learns who people are: nobody has a label.
  - Graham wants your name spelt correctly for the membership list.
  - Coach Pemberton watches from the side, arms folded, and says very little.
  - Four placement games: Marjorie, Dex, Graham, Clive. Pemberton gives you your starting rating like it's a verdict.
  - Toby, also new, asks for a quick one before they stack the chairs, "just for fun". He takes you apart. "Beginner's luck. Mine, I mean. Want to go over it?"
  - **Seed:** Pemberton watched Toby's game over his shoulder the whole time. He didn't watch yours.
- **Seeds:** the honours board on the wall. One name keeps coming up as club champion, year after year, and then stops: V. Hart **[?]**. Marjorie: "Board two. Honestly. Thirty years." (Vera left when the coach put her on board two; the player isn't told that yet.)

### Chapter 1: Marjorie · "First proper club night"

- Marjorie takes you under her wing: tea, gossip, and her London System.
- She's lovely. She also beats you in an endgame you thought was drawn, and apologises while doing it.
- **After the match:** "Well done. I'd have played on, but it's nearly ten."
- **Seed:** she mentions the club used to fill two rooms.

### Chapter 2: Dex · "The streamer"

- A teenager with a phone propped against a water bottle. "It's not recording. Probably."
- He plays gambits and talks like the internet. He's annoying and, against your will, funny.
- **After you beat him:** "Don't clip that." (It is, of course, being clipped.)
- **Seed:** his viewer count goes up when you play him. He doesn't mention why.

### Chapter 3: Oscar (and Neil) · "Junior night"

- Oscar is eleven, polite, silent and very quick. Neil, his dad, hovers.
- Neil has an excuse ready for every result and a boast for every other moment.
- **Beat:** Oscar is the only person who shakes your hand without looking at you or saying anything, and somehow it's the most respectful thing all week.

### Chapter 4: Clive · "A quiet one"

- A retired engineer with a flask who offers a draw at move 12 every single time.
- **The lesson in story form:** Clive thinks wanting to win is slightly vulgar. Beating him means making a game out of nothing.
- **Seed:** Clive mentions he played board one "back when there was a proper board one." He changes the subject.

### Chapter 5: Priya · "By the book"

- She's done every course and read every book. She knows twenty moves of theory, then stares at the board for twenty minutes.
- **Her small arc starts here:** after you beat her, she asks, quietly, "So what do you actually do when the book stops?"

### Chapter 6: Graham · "Subs are due"

- The treasurer and rules stickler. Scoresheets, both copies.
- **The club's problem surfaces:** Graham has done a spreadsheet. The Red Lion wants more for the room. The numbers don't work.
- He doesn't make a drama of it. That's what makes it worrying.

### Chapter 7: Toby · "No pressure, mate"

- Your first proper game against him since trial night. He's on the membership list now, with a rating like everyone else.
- You've seen him beat everyone you've beaten, slightly faster, and always call it luck.
- He offers to "go over games together." He sends you his Lichess study. He's helpful. You can't justify disliking him.
- **Beat:** Coach Pemberton's scouting report on Toby is oddly thin, compared with the ones on everyone else. **[?]**

### The knockout cup: "The club's oldest trophy"

- Three rounds against club regulars (Oscar, Clive, Graham), then the final.
- **Final:** Toby. Marjorie sets out the good set, the one without the mismatched knight.
- **If you lose:** "Good game! Want to go over it?" and Toby gets more supportive with every rematch, which is worse. Qualify again next month.
- **If you win:** Toby: "So deserved. Genuinely made up for you." Marjorie engraves the cup ("Well, the engraver needed it by Friday"). Pemberton congratulates you, then asks Toby to stay behind.
- **Act 1 closes on:** the noticeboard: a new sheet, "League team trials: the club ladder." Toby's name is already at the top.

## Key story points to review

These shape the rest of the story. Each has my recommendation; change anything.

### 1. Who is the player?

Do you have a name, and do you ever speak?

**Recommendation:**
- The player is a silent main character, as in Pokémon.
- Graham asks for your name on trial night ("spelt properly") and characters use it. It's the only thing the player types.
- No dialogue choices.

### 2. How the scouting-report betrayal is revealed (Act 2)

**Recommendation:** Toby sends you "his Lichess study" as usual, but it's the wrong one. It's called "Prep: [your name]" and it's full of Pemberton's notes on your weaknesses. It uses a line he's been saying since Act 1.
- **Toby:** "Oh. That's just general stuff. Everyone does prep, mate."
- **Pemberton:** "I coach everyone. It's only fair."

### 3. How the club is saved (Act 3)

The room hire needs money a league season alone wouldn't bring in.

**Recommendation:** Dex's stream brings in new members. People who watched "fork guy" start turning up on club nights, and their subs cover the room.
- Dex never admits he did it on purpose. Graham updates the spreadsheet without comment.
- This ties Dex's arc to the club's survival and makes the stream reveal pay off.

### 4. Why Toby leaves for Kingsbridge

**Recommendation:** Kingsbridge offers him board two in a higher division. He frames it as "growth" and "a new challenge". He never tells you himself; Marjorie does.
- Vera's verdict: "He'll do. Board three." She's put him lower than he was promised, which delights everyone at Wexley.

### 5. Pemberton's ending

The tone guide says he never admits a mistake.

**Recommendation:** no apology and no redemption speech.
- His comeuppance is quiet. When Vera comes back, board one is hers, and he has to write it on the team sheet himself. He pauses before her name, as always.

### 6. Vera's return (the final arc)

**Recommendation:**
- **Act 3:** beating her in the county cup final plants it. "Well played. I've not been sleeping."
- **Act 4:** she turns up at congresses and watches your games without comment.
- **After the final congress:** she's at the Red Lion on a club night: "Is the urn still broken?" The story ends there, with the next season's team sheet on the noticeboard.

### 7. Where the story ends

Act 4 is open-ended: new cities can keep being added.

**Recommendation:** the final congress and the Vera beat are the story's ending, with credits. Any cities added afterwards are post-game: no new story, just new opponents and the rival levels.
